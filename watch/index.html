TRANSCRIPT

Read along

00:00

Make room for reading.

Pocket Daily is a quiet e-book reader for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Start with one book, without a Pocket Daily account or a separate reader.

00:10

A library of your own.

Add DRM-free EPUB, text or Markdown files to the Library. Adjust type size, spacing and page color to make reading comfortable.

00:20

Keep a good article.

Save a good article from the share sheet, or subscribe to an RSS feed. Read saved text offline and send a selected article to your reader when you choose.

00:30

Connect when you choose.

Connect a compatible X3 or X4 running Pocket Daily firmware on the same Wi-Fi. Direct connections to factory firmware are not supported.

00:41

The same book. Your place.

Keep the same book file on each device. Share reading places through your own iCloud between Apple devices, or locally with a connected reader. You choose whether to jump.

00:52

A reader that feels like yours.

Preview and apply Home and Sleep layouts. Add only the cards, weather and events you want. Demo screens are previews: they never change a device or send files.

01:04

Make time for the next page.

The guide takes you from your first book to connections and troubleshooting. Transferring firmware is not installation: you must confirm separately on the reader.

Availability

This page publishes the introduction and guide. It does not distribute app or firmware binaries or announce App Store availability. Full reader companion features need supported Pocket Daily firmware.
