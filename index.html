Before connecting a reader

The app reads books without hardware. Full companion features require compatible ESP32-C3 X3/X4 hardware running Pocket Daily firmware. Ordinary CrossPoint supports discovery and explicit migration guidance; factory firmware cannot connect directly. X4 Classic/V2 and X4 Pro are different models. Check the exact model and installation eligibility first.
