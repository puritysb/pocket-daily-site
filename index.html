Compatibility and connection guide ↗Troubleshooting and support
Before buying or installing

App reading needs no reader. Full companion features need supported ESP32-C3 X3/X4 hardware running Pocket Daily firmware. Ordinary CrossPoint is limited to discovery and explicit migration guidance in this app. Factory firmware cannot connect directly; X4 Classic/V2 and X4 Pro are different models. Confirm the exact model, installation eligibility and recovery method first.
